Security

Protection you can inspect.

Security is a product requirement. The claims below describe how Mampi Technologies Ltd actually builds and operates its software.

Encrypted transport

Public properties are served over HTTPS. Vortex9 speaks to mystic9.net only on TLS 1.2 or TLS 1.3 and refuses plain HTTP.

Local records stay local

Vortex9 stores sensitive on-device records with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is wrapped by the operating system and is not sent to mystic9.net.

Local-first privacy

Vortex9 keeps scans, block decisions, and household rules on the device. Activation sends the account email, and a receipt reference only if you provide one, so the app can confirm a subscription.
