Who may use Decoy9

You must be 18 or older. The website asks you to confirm that age before the Windows download. The app itself has no age screen. You will not create a peer relationship with a child, and you will not use Decoy9 to contact a minor.

If you see conduct of that kind, stop the peer on your device and write to support@mampitech.com. Where the law requires a report to the police or another authority, make that report. The company will co-operate with a lawful request that it is actually able to answer.

On-device controls

Decoy9 is an encrypted vault for your private notes. Everything stays on your device, with no accounts, no telemetry and no cloud. Peer-to-peer messaging is in development. This build has no message path and does not scan note contents. Safety controls run on the device.

  • An unknown public key waits in Pending Requests. Until you accept it, inbound text, links, and media from that key are not shown.
  • Decline blocks that key on this device.
  • After you accept a peer, remote addresses in their messages stay replaced with a held-link mark until you choose to inspect them. The client does not open the address for you.
  • Those decisions stay on the device. They are not uploaded into a moderation queue.

The company cannot read the vault in order to police it, because the notes stay encrypted on the device and are not sent to the company. You are the person who accepts a key, and you are the person who must refuse one.

What you store

You alone choose what you seal on this device. You will not use Decoy9 to infringe another person’s rights, to impersonate Mampi Technologies Ltd, or to interfere with the client. The Terms of Service state that you are responsible for every note you store on the device.
