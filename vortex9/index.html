Vortex9

Security Policy

This policy covers the Vortex9 desktop app only. It is separate from the mystic9.net sanctuary terms.

Encryption at rest

Local records use AES-256-GCM. That includes the subscription cache, Guardian settings, household keywords and blocked sites, and the saved threat-feed host list. The encryption key is wrapped by the operating system and is not sent to mystic9.net. If a record cannot be decrypted, the app does not treat it as valid.

Packaged binary

The Windows and macOS installers pack the app into an ASAR archive and enable Electron’s archive integrity check. A changed archive does not load. The check applies to that packaged build. A development launch from source is not the fused installer.

The window

The interface runs in a sandboxed page with context isolation. It cannot open a shell, start a new window, or follow an outside address on its own. Shield actions stay in the main process.

Transport

The app speaks to mystic9.net only over HTTPS. Those connections require TLS 1.2 or TLS 1.3 and a certificate the device trusts. A plain HTTP address is refused, and a license redirect is refused.

Vortex9

Guardian Protocol Terms

Guardian Protocol is a household filter inside the paid Vortex9 shield. These terms are separate from the mystic9.net sanctuary terms.

Who controls it

The operator creates a passphrase on the device. That passphrase is stored only as a slow hash, and it is not sent to mystic9.net. Changing the watch switch or opening household rules requires that passphrase, or device sign-in where the Mac provides it. Five wrong attempts pause confirmation for 15 minutes. A confirmation token works once and expires after two minutes.

What it checks

Three lists run in one local check. The baseline list is built into the app and cannot be edited: adult-content indicators, tracking hosts, and predatory-host blocks. An optional threat feed adds host names the operator chooses to download. Household keywords and blocked sites are added by the operator and stored encrypted on the device.

What it does not do

Guardian does not decrypt secure web traffic and does not keep a browsing log. A blocked address is refused as a tunnel. Pasted text, and the clipboard only when the operator turns watch on, is checked on the device. A match adds a category count. The text itself is not stored.

The household

The filter is a tool for the adult who runs the device. It does not replace supervision. The free shield leaves Guardian locked.
